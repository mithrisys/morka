# Morka branding

- `morka-logo.png`: approved transparent logo and wordmark.
- `morka-icon.png`: icon adaptation of that logo, with the symbol on a warm white macOS tile.
- `Morka.icns`: the icon encoded as PNG representations at 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512 and 1024 pixels, including Retina representations.
- `generated-app-icon.png` supplies the single-arch, single-bud mark for generated apps and their clones. Each new app randomly chooses one of eight colors for the mark and its glow on the launcher’s cream tile. A clone draws from the other seven colors. The project stores the chosen icon for future builds.

The launcher uses `Morka.icns` in the macOS bundle and `morka-icon.png` in the development Dock. Artwork was generated with the built-in **ImageGen** tool.

## Icon adaptation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Edit target: the attached approved Morka logo. Make a production macOS app icon using EXACTLY the existing violet symbol from this image. Remove ONLY the wordmark 'morka', retain the symbol's exact silhouette, three rounded lower legs, arches, two budding round tips, orientation, proportions, and violet color. Do not redesign or reinterpret it. Center the existing symbol, scaled with comfortable padding, on a clean warm-white rounded-square macOS icon tile. Square canvas 1024x1024; the rounded-square tile fills about 88% width and height with consistent transparent margin OUTSIDE the tile. Symbol fills about 67% canvas width. Crisp smooth edges and flat solid violet; plain warm white tile; transparent outside tile. No text, no new objects, no new shapes within symbol, no watermark, no perspective or mockup. This is an adaptation of an approved logo, preserve its identity faithfully.

## Final refinement prompt

Precisely clean up this macOS app icon. Keep the existing violet organic Morka symbol and its proportions, placement and colors identical. Keep the warm-white rounded square tile. ## Generated app icon prompt

Retain the same flat deep violet ink, smooth organic contours, substantial round-ended legs, cream squircle and optical scale. The parent silhouette has two lower arches, three feet, and two budding tips. For the junior design draw exactly ONE substantial upside-down U arch, with TWO blunt round-ended feet defining a generous open counter. Straight-on square app icon on a transparent margin, no sample palette, no lettering.

## Glow refinement prompt

Micro-edit the attached approved Morka child application icon. Keep its existing single arch, two feet and one round bud, exact violet silhouette, dimensions, placement, cream rounded-square tile and transparent outer margin. Add only a restrained, softly diffused lavender ambient glow on the cream immediately outside the purple shape, following its entire contour including inside the arch. At 1024 px the glow falls off smoothly over about 22 px, strongest closest to the ink. Aim for 12% violet tint on the adjacent cream, nearly invisible farther out. Keep the ink a rich solid violet, crisp. No stroke, white rim, additional symbols, shadow, bevel or gloss. Same square, straight-on production icon at identical framing.
